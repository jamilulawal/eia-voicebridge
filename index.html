🌍 What VoiceBridge Examines

Key environmental and community dimensions of an EIA.

💧 Water

Potential effects on rivers, groundwater, drainage, water quality and water resources.

🌫️ Air Quality

Dust, emissions, smoke, pollutants and possible effects on surrounding communities.

🌱 Land & Soil

Soil disturbance, erosion, contamination, land-use change and degradation.

🐘 Biodiversity

Potential effects on vegetation, wildlife, habitats and ecological systems.

👥 Community

Community health, livelihoods, social impacts, safety and affected populations.

🛡️ Mitigation

Measures proposed to prevent, reduce or manage environmental and social impacts.
